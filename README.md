# Hi, I'm Amin 👋

🔬 **I do explainable AI** — which mostly means asking neural networks to show their work, and
then checking whether the explanation they give is actually true. Turns out models are very
good at being right for reasons nobody can verify, and I find that unreasonably interesting.

Right now I'm finishing an M.Sc. in Automation and Control at RPTU Kaiserslautern, doing
research at **Fraunhofer IOSB** in Karlsruhe, and previously spent a year and a half at **DFKI**.

## 🧪 What I'm working on

**Master's thesis** — *A Comparative Evaluation of Explainable AI Methods for Graph Neural
Network-based Remaining Useful Life Prediction.*

Jet engines degrade. Graph neural networks can predict when they'll fail. But *why* did the
model say 47 cycles and not 90? I'm putting four explainers — GNNExplainer, PGExplainer, SHAP
and Integrated Gradients — through the same wringer on NASA's C-MAPSS data and scoring them on
fidelity, stability and whether two independently trained models tell you the same story.
Spoiler: explanations that look convincing and explanations that are faithful are not the
same set. 🙃

**Fraunhofer IOSB** — explainable AI, with the Vision and Fusion Laboratory at KIT.

## 🤖 Things I've built

**[Project_Lab_CAS](https://github.com/AminDaryan/Project_Lab_CAS)** — teaching a 7-DOF Franka
Emika Panda to find a randomly placed object and pick it up without knocking anything over.
ROS, MoveIt/OMPL, a YOLOv5 I trained on a dataset I filmed and annotated myself, and a
RealSense D455 turning pixels into 3D poses. Four of us built it; the perception half is mine.

**👁️ Eye movement classification on a HoloLens 2** — my master's project at DFKI. I put an AR
headset on ten people, had them walk, wait, glue things and assemble things in a smart factory,
and asked whether you can tell what someone is doing purely from how their eyes move. (Mostly
yes.) Unity + MRTK + ARETT for recording, 19 gaze features, seven classifiers in a bake-off.
